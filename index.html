Recreation

Words about the project from the author

This ia a recreation of a universe I made during high school. In high school I created my first fictional universe at my first college I created my second. The first one I made in high school was saved on a google folder that I accidentally deleted with no other back ups. In many ways that universe died and will never be brought back. The second universe I created at my first college was very different as I had no intention of creating the same thing twice. And what I learned from my experience from the first universe was not to save backups but instead save the universe in the on;ly place that I knew I could never delete. I memorized the entire universe, magic system, government system, history, characters, all of it. But that limited the scope of what I could create. Now rather than create something completely new, rather than save it all in my head, rather than recreate something I’ve already done I am going to tell a story, maybe more creating a third universe that draws inspiration from my first two and mixing in something new.

To share a bit of what I see this project being: I am keeping a public journal of my thoughts to work through some life events. I often find myself writing technical reports and documents for educational purposes or work. I have a poetry page which holds poetry I write. But this is an exercise in writing fiction and creating universes. In short, it is a break from technical or deep writing, and is just me and my imagination without restrictions.

Prologue

Cold. For all the energy in the galaxy, for all the heat thrown off by the dying stars, the sky still felt cold. The Young One, Phoenix, and Draygon flew threw the cold towards a blue ball. The blue planet of massive size grew as the three of them approach. How could such a large hub of life be so cold? The Young One wondered as they entered the upper atmosphere. Typically plants with atmospheres where warmer than space, or at least one that supported life. And there was so much life on this planet. Or at least according to the great records this planet was a hub for life. But looking down on the seas of blue hughes all that the three tituses felt was a void.

“The cataclysm must have already claimed this one.” Draygon physically communicated “we’ll check the pillar and move to the next one.”

“By your command.” The others thought in return.

Coming to the surface The Young One was surprised to see not oceans but hills of blue and turquoise grasses. In fact their wasn’t any liquid to be seen. But there in the distance stood the pillar. A cracked collum emanating an aura of shadow.

“Of course the pillar was visible only to those tuned into the essence of existence. The species of this planet almost certainly could perceive there was a pillar of the universe on their planet. But why was it cracked? What in the universe could even crack a pillar of the universe?” The Young One thought.

“Nothing from this universe.” Phoenix interjected. “And keep your thoughts clear, we need this line of communication open Young One.”

“Right, I’ll stay focused” The Young One replied. This was their first mission. And what a way to start. Most Tituses started off making contact with a spices and being worshiped as gods. But to start out by finding a broken pillar, a lifeless living planet. Events like this haven’t happened since the great war. Or at least according to the great records. Then again, even during the great war never saw the existence of the universe be threatened. After all it was only the pillars that held up the expanse, that held back the collapse of everything. But what even happens if the universe collapses? Whats left? Nothing? A void? It didn’t matter, that was above The Young Ones station.

As the three of them set foot in the temple of the pillar of the universe a draft began to pick up. Suddenly it ripped Phoenix off her feet. Draygon grabbed her hand without hesitation, be even he, a great master titus was struggling to hold his footing. The emptiness on the other side of the crack was allconsuming.

“We have no choice, we must take our stand here.” Phoenix yelled at the two of them.

“She’s right, it’s time. The end has come, and now we must ensure the next universe has protectors as well. Young One, you’ve learned your lessons well?” Draygon asked as though there was anything anyone could do about it now.

They began the process. Draygon let Phenix go and she thew herself at the crack. Using her very essence as a plug capping the hole in the universe. The air was calm. But Phoenix began to strain herself. The Young One didn’t even know that tituses could exert themselves, but he supposed that he also never considered a titus being torn apart, and so extremes meet or what ever. Draygon was hard at work performing the construction of some king of projection device. It was hard to make out.

But it became clear real quick as he began siphoning what was left of Phoenix's essence, then just as he aim the projector in line with the crack with The Young One standing in between, Draygon emptied his own energy into the projector an shot pushing The Young One into the cracked pillar.

A flash and then nothing. The Young One was left floating in an abyss. Alone.

“Am I dead? Can I die? Where am I? What happed? Who am I? Where do I come from?” Thoughts left floating through the empty abyss. A consciousness that seemed familiar to itself but out of place tried to make sense of the perceptionless existence.

Moments turned to years turned to centuries until time had no meaning. Of course meaningless time seemed to fit as it hadn’t been discovered yet. Or maybe it had been forgotten. It was so hard to tell. But eventually the isolated presence wasn’t alone. There was another. Kin, formless yet present.

Intertangled and entwined they became as over the seemingly infinite time to come eventually, one by one five entities, five presences formless and connected. Each an identity, each unique, but none isolated, none severed from the collective. And together an era of focus dawned. Progress was slow at first but as it will always be, momentum builds, and eventually for the first time in this new existence form took shape. The five that became one became five. The first five. And with their puncture of substance through the void came an explosion of substance. Not only did the first five now have form, but so to did everything else. Expanding, exploding, obliterating the nothingness that came from before, and the cycle of existence continued anew.
